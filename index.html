Our Commitment

Living lightly
on the land
we love

Sustainability isn't a policy here — it's the reason we exist. From the way we build to the food we grow, every decision asks: does this leave the land better than we found it?

01
Ecobricks

Non-recyclable plastic packed into repurposed bottles and used as durable building blocks — keeping waste out of the land and on-site.

02
Permaculture & Food Forests

Regenerative gardens designed to give back. Food forests, composting, and organic growing feed our community while restoring the soil beneath us.

03
Natural Building

Bamboo, earth, and reclaimed materials. Every structure here is a collaboration between human hands and the materials the land freely offers.

04
Rainwater Harvesting

Krabi's rainfall is a gift. We collect, filter, and store water across the site — closing the cycle and reducing our dependency on outside systems.

05
Zero Waste Ethos

From kitchen scraps to packaging, nothing goes to landfill if we can help it. Sorting, composting, and upcycling turn waste into resource.

06
Leave No Trace Climbing

Climbers are stewards first. We maintain our crags with deep respect for the rock and the ecosystems around them — every route taken with care.

Our Story

About Us

Who We Are

Born from the rock,
built by community

Spirit Mountain began as a climbers' camp at the base of a stunning limestone formation in Chongpli, Krabi, Thailand. Over the years it has grown into something far more — a living community space where adventure, nature, and human connection intersect.

The land holds ancient caves, wild jungle, and some of the best sport climbing in Southern Thailand. We didn't build Spirit Mountain — we listened to the mountain, and let it guide us.

Today, Spirit Mountain hosts climbers, families, artists, healers, farmers, and wanderers. Our doors are open to anyone who arrives with an open heart.

The Crag

Southern Thailand's
finest limestone

Spirit Mountain sits at the base of a dramatic limestone formation rising out of the jungle in Chongpli, Krabi. The crag offers 30+ bolted sport routes from beginner 5a to a challenging 7c+.

Featured limestone with pockets, crimps, slopers, and dynamic movement. Routes are well-bolted and carefully maintained. A short jungle walk leads you to the wall.

We offer guided sessions for all levels, gear rental, and a relaxed community atmosphere for experienced climbers.

The Routes

Something for every level

Beginner · 5a–5c
First Touch

Juggy limestone, generous rests, and friendly pitches. Perfect for families and first-timers. All gear included.

Intermediate · 6a–7a
Flow State

Athletic routes with sustained movement and technical footwork. The sweet spot for climbers wanting to push their game.

Advanced · 7a+–7c+
The Test Piece

Steep, powerful, and technical. Krabi's best limestone challenges, waiting for the bold.

Guided Sessions

All abilities welcome. Experienced guides, half-day and full-day options. Gear included. Just show up.
